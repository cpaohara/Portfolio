About Me

Hi, I'm Ciaran. I'm a undergraduate student currently studying Computer Games Programming at De Montfort University, Leicester. I was first introduced to programming at secondary school, and it was one of my favourite things to do in the subject. Because of this, I studied Computer Science at A Level, and had the chance to create a game for my coursework, which was the Minesweeper made in PyGame in Python. I enjoy programming, and learning of new ways to code, and am wanting to expand my knowledge of languages I know, and those that I am yet to learn. Below is a portfolio of my best work, and each entry will have an image carousel of the project, and files to the source code and their GDDs and TDDs if they have them.
